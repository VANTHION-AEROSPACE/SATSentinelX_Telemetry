# IAAI claims and limitations

## We can claim
- SATSentinel-X reconstructs and analyzes real ESA Mission-1 telemetry and event metadata.
- The evaluated Class-3 population is multivariate and morphologically heterogeneous.
- Frozen v1 detected all 30 evaluated Class-3 events in both recorded ESA evaluation settings.
- The broader label-negative stress evaluation produced 23.5% FPR, 100% recall, and strong ranking metrics.
- v2 was a controlled experimental comparison that did not resolve the principal failure modes.
- OPSSAT exposed protocol/domain-transfer limitations.

## We should claim carefully
The strong-only point is a secondary pre-existing operating point, not an optimized or independently validated threshold. ROC-AUC/PR-AUC describe ranking separation and do not imply low FPR at the primary operating point.

## We must not claim
The 170 controls are normal or nominal; OPSSAT validates v1; v2 is the final detector; the system is operationally deployable; generalization is proven; or Detector v3 is justified.

All causal explanations for false positives remain hypotheses.
