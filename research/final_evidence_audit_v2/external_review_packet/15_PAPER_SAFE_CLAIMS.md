# Paper-safe claims

## A. SAFE TO SAY

- Frozen Detector v1 detected all 30 evaluated Class-3 events under the stated primary rule.
- The detector detected 40 of 170 predetermined label-negative/unlabelled control periods.
- Detector score was strongly associated with channel dimensionality in the examined control population.
- Class-3 positives were shorter than controls in the evaluated population.
- Class-3 positives showed a moderate-direction shift toward higher channel counts.
- The specific hypothesis that the 30/30 result was explained by substantially higher dimensionality among Class-3 positives was weakened, but structural comparability remained limited.
- These analyses are descriptive and do not establish causation.
- OPSSAT-AD was used as a protocol/domain stress test rather than direct frozen-v1 validation.
- The system was demonstrated through actual Yamcs 5.13.2 ground-system integration.

## B. MUST QUALIFY

30/30, 40/170, ROC-AUC 0.9924, the strong-only 1.18% control-detection point, Yamcs 5/6, the OPSSAT result, and all generalization claims. State the population, protocol, denominator, and limitation each time.

## C. MUST NOT SAY

100% accuracy; low or confirmed false-positive rate; broad generalization; OPSSAT validation; operational or flight validation; flight readiness; six-of-six Yamcs equivalence; dimensionality causes false positives; duration causes false positives; or v2 outperforms v1.
