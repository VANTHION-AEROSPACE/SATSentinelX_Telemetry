# Final duration–dimensionality population comparison

## Population
30 established Class-3 positives and 170 predetermined `LABEL_NEGATIVE_UNLABELLED` controls. No detector outputs were used to select or remove observations.

## Methodology
Existing frozen structural features and existing duration/channel bins were compared. Summaries, distribution overlap, standardized effects, Mann–Whitney tests and stratified counts were computed descriptively.

## Results
Class-3 positives were shorter than controls: Hedges g=-0.609713, Mann–Whitney p=4.97e-8, overlap=0.621569. Channel count showed a modest positive shift: Hedges g=0.210893, Mann–Whitney p=0.0120, overlap=0.390196. Existing channel bins were 11/104, 17/32 and 2/34 for positives/controls in 1–6, 7–12 and 13–26 channels respectively.

## Interpretation
The specific hypothesis that 30/30 is explained by substantially higher dimensionality among Class-3 positives is weakened. Structural comparability remains limited, so the residual structural-confound classification is MODERATE. The canonical 40/170=23.5294% control-detection result is unchanged. These findings are descriptive and do not establish that duration or dimensionality causes detections.

## Limitations
The positive population is small; controls are not proven nominal; the comparison is observational; and the result does not establish broad accuracy or eliminate structural confounding. No detector modification is justified.

## Paper-safe wording
“Class-3 positives were shorter than the broader control population and showed a modest shift toward higher channel counts. The specific hypothesis that the 30/30 result was explained by substantially higher dimensionality among Class-3 positives was weakened, but structural comparability remained limited. These analyses are descriptive and do not establish causation.”
